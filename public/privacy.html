Your records. Your storage.

Kairo Ride is a browser-based ride archive for odometer history, rides, multi-day trips, vehicles, gear, maintenance and personal analytics. This page describes how this version handles data.

Where data is stored

Records and attached originals are first saved in this device's browser IndexedDB. If you connect Google Drive, they sync to a private Kairo Ride folder in your Google account. Kairo Ride has no separate personal-data backend.

The hosting service serves the website code (GitHub Pages in the standard deployment). Hosting and Google services may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy terms. Your odometer entries, notes and attachments are not sent to the GitHub repository.

Google permission

Kairo Ride requests the drive.file scope. It can work only with files the app creates or that you explicitly make available to it; it does not request access to read your entire Drive. Your email address and Google permission ID distinguish accounts and show which account is connected.

The temporary access token remains in the memory of the open app window. It is not written to backups, GitHub or persistent local storage. Reloading the app or token expiry requires refreshing access. Sign-in and Drive requests go directly to Google.

After you connect Drive, automatic sync is enabled by default on this device. While the app is visible, online and authorized, it uploads saved changes and checks for updates about once per minute, as well as when you return or reconnect. You can disable this in Settings and use Sync now. Synchronization uses your network connection and Drive storage; it is not guaranteed while the app is hidden or closed.

Files and backups

GPX files, logs, photos and videos upload without modifying the original content. JSON and Excel exports contain record history and attachment metadata, but not the attachment originals. The Drive folder is not made public automatically; its owner can change sharing permissions in Drive.

Deletion and device security

Deleting a record creates a history event so earlier revisions remain recoverable. Removing an attachment link does not delete the original Drive file. To remove everything, delete the Kairo Ride folder and copies in Drive, clear this site's data on every device, and delete your exports.

Signing out does not erase local data. Account copies on this device are separated but are not protected by an additional Kairo Ride password or application-level encryption. Protect your device and avoid private records in a shared browser profile.

You can revoke Google access in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not itself delete files already created.

Analytics, reminders and advertising

The Analytics tab calculates charts locally from your own odometer history. It is not usage tracking and sends no telemetry. Kairo Ride contains no advertising or background location tracking. Optional maintenance notifications are generated locally while the PWA is open or active.

If you deploy the project under your own GitHub account, you operate that copy. Before offering it publicly, replace this section with the operator name and a valid support contact.
